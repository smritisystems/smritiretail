# 4. Purchase Order Creation

## Goal

Prepare a Purchase Order (PO) that clearly states what the business wants to buy and from which vendor.

## Who Can Do This?

Purchase Studio is listed for `MANAGER` and `SYSADMIN` users. Ask for the correct role if the tile is missing.

## Steps

```mermaid
flowchart LR
  O[Open Purchase Studio] --> V[Select vendor]
  V --> W[Select warehouse]
  W --> I[Add approved items]
  I --> Q[Enter quantity and rate]
  Q --> C[Check commercial terms and total]
  C --> D[Save Draft]
  D --> R[Review and print preview]
  R --> S[Submit PO when approved]
```

1. Open **Purchase Studio** from the Launchpad.
2. Start a new PO. Confirm the document is a **Purchase Order**, not an Indent or another document type.
3. Select the correct vendor. Check the vendor name and code.
4. Select the receiving warehouse/delivery location. If the warehouse list cannot load, stop and ask the trainer; do not save with a guessed location.
5. Add each approved item from the catalog. Check the item code, description, and vendor policy result.
6. Enter the ordered quantity and purchase rate from the approved request or quotation.
7. Review tax, discounts, freight, payment terms, and delivery terms. Use **Commercial Terms** when those details need to be entered.
8. Check the PO total and all line quantities.
9. Click **Save Draft** to keep a draft for review.
10. Click **Preview (F9)** or **Print PO** to review the document. Check vendor, delivery location, items, quantities, rates, tax, and total.
11. After the trainer or authorized approver checks it, click **Submit PO** and follow any approval prompts.
12. Write down the PO number and status. You will need the PO when goods arrive.

## Important

- A saved draft is not necessarily an approved PO. Check its status before telling a vendor to ship.
- If an item is marked blocked or requires approval, follow the on-screen explanation. Do not bypass it.
- Do not submit a practice PO in a live company.

## Practice Task

Create a draft PO in the training company using the assigned vendor, warehouse, item, quantity, and rate. Preview it and explain the total before submitting.
