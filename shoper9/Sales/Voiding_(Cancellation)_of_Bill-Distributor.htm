Voiding (Cancellation) of Invoice

The Void Invoice is used to cancel an already settled invoice, which is invoiced on the current day of operations. Only those invoices that are settled on the current Shoper date can be voided. Once the day close operations are performed, then the invoices made on the previous Shoper date cannot be voided.

The Void Invoice window is displayed.

Invoice Doc.Pfx.: Enter the Document Prefix of the invoice to be cancelled.

Invoice Doc.No.: Enter the Document Number of the invoice to be cancelled. If the invoice is available in the system the details of the invoice get displayed. Otherwise, an error message prompts the non-availability of the invoice for voiding.

Reason Code: Select the reason for the cancellation of the invoice, from the drop down list.

Void Invoice Date and Time: The Shoper date is displayed, which is considered as the cancelation date of the invoice. The system time is displayed next to the Shoper date.

Bill Type: The invoice type (Product/ Service) of the document number entered is displayed in this field, i.e., the invoice being cancelled is a Product Invoice or Service Invoice is displayed. Also, the transaction type of the invoice (Cash/ Credit) is displayed in the next field.

Sales Staff: Sales man ID of the salesperson who was involved in the invoicing is displayed.

Note: The cancellation of invoices will be listed in the sales-man register and the cancellation value will be reduced from the net sale value for the sales staff.

Customer Code: The customer code of the invoice being cancelled is displayed.   

Customer Name: The customer name of the invoice being cancelled is displayed.

Void Reference

Doc. Prefix: The prefix set for void invoice gets displayed automatically.

Doc No.: The document number for the void invoice is generated automatically.

Item Details Grid

The item details of the document number selected for cancellation get displayed in the Item Details grid.

Invoice Details Grid

This grid displays the invoice details like total sale value, total discounts, total Addons, total deductions and total sales tax.

Quantity: The quantity of items in the invoice being cancelled is displayed.

Net-Amount: The net amount of the invoice being cancelled is displayed.

Ok: To confirm the cancellation of the invoice

Cancel: To terminate the cancellation of the invoice

 

Note: On cancelling a settled invoice which included a Sales Order/ Sales DC/ Approval Issue DC, then the status of the Sales Order/ Sales DC/ Approval Issue DC is reinstated and can be used later, depending on the parameter setting.
