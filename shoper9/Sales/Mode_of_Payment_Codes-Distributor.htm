Mode of Payment

The payment (tender) details are mandatory for any invoice settlement. When you settle a Cash Invoice using F8, you can settle the invoice using different payment modes including cash.

Note: On recalling a suspended invoice with payment details entry, the details will be displayed in the Payment Details pop up.

In a Cash Invoice, if the customer wants to settle the invoice using different payment modes, including cash, the same can be recorded in the payment details grid, which pops up on pressing F8 from the Invoice window. For example, an invoice of Rs. 5000/- can be settled by paying Rs. 2000/- by Cheque, Rs. 2500/- by ON-ACC and the balance by cash.

In a Cash Invoice, on pressing F8 from Invoice window, the Mode of Payment/ Payment Details pops up in the Invoice window.

Mode of Payment: Shoper 9 POS (Distributor) allows the following payment modes to be enabled. Select the required mode of payment.

  • Cash

  • Cheque

  • Credit Note

Note: For any of the above payment modes to be available in invoicing, the same needs to be catalogued under Currency Catalogue.

On selecting the mode of payment, the various corresponding codes catalogued for a particular mode of payment get listed in the adjacent field. Select the required code and the relevant details to be captured are displayed.

For Cash: Select the relevant currency code, if foreign currencies are defined, from the drop down list.

No. Of Units: The receivable amount is displayed by default.

Exchg. Rate: The exchange rate as defined in the Currency Catalogue is displayed.

Amount: The amount [No. Of Units x Exchg.Rate] is displayed. You can alter the amount, if required. The amount entered in this field is considered as the Number of Units.

The payment details like Payment Mode, Payment Code, Number of Units, Exchange Rate, Amount and Description are displayed in the respective fields.

Total Qty: The total quantity/ number of items selected for invoicing is displayed.

Net Payable: The net amount that the customer has to pay is displayed.

Amount Receivable: The amount receivable is displayed. Initially, it shows the net invoice value. As the tender details are recorded, only the balance payable is displayed.

Note: If you enter more than the receivable amount in the Amount field, the balance amount along with a minus sign is displayed in the Amount Receivable field. This facility is available only for Cash mode.

The payment details entered by Cash mode, where the amount entered is more than the receivable amount, is displayed.

Payment details for other modes: For other modes, the additional information to be captured are as defined in their respective catalogues.

For Cheque: Select the required payment code from the drop down list.

Enter Cheque Number and Cheque Date.

Amount: The receivable amount is displayed. You can alter the amount, if required. You cannot enter more than the receivable amount.

The payment details like Payment Mode, Payment Code, Amount and Description (Cheque Number/ Cheque Date) are displayed in the respective fields.

For Credit Note: Select the required payment code from the drop down list.

The invoice settlement by Credit Note varies according to the parameter setting, except in those invoice settlements where the invoice amount is same as the selected Credit Note amount. Click here to know how to set the respective parameters.

Enter the Credit Note Prefix and Credit Note number.

Amount: The amount in the selected Credit Note is displayed. You can alter the amount, if required. You cannot enter more than the Credit Note amount. Also, you cannot enter more than the receivable amount.

The payment details like Payment Mode, Payment Code, Amount and Description (Credit Note Prefix/ Credit Note Number) are displayed in the respective fields.

Note: The amount in a Credit Note can be used in more than one invoice settlement, till the Credit Note amount gets exhausted.

 

The Invoice window with the payment details is displayed.

Note: The Payment Details grid displays all the payment modes along with the details, used for the invoice settlement. You can delete all or any of the payments, if required.

To delete a payment detail entered, you can use any of the following methods:

   · Right click on the payment detail and select Delete Payment Details

   · Select the payment detail by clicking and then press F4 when it is high lighted

 

Note: Press Esc to add more items to the invoice/ recall any document, after capturing payment details. You have a choice to either retain the payment details entered or delete it.

After retaining the payment details, you have two options to proceed with the invoice settlement:

   · Press F8: The payment details entered previously get displayed and you can enter the payment details for the remaining invoice amount.

   · Press F7: You are alerted with a message stating that payment details exists and you have a choice to replace the existing payment details with Cash.

 

Ok: To accept the entries made towards payment or press F10 to save. On saving an invoice, you can do the following, if configured:

   · Print the invoice

   · Print a Delivery Challan for the invoice

   · Create a PT file for the invoice

Cancel: To exit the payment details grid. You have a choice to either retain the payment details entered or delete it.
