Reprint Sales DC

Sales DC generated earlier can be reprinted using this option. The reprinting of a Sales DC is required, if it is lost or damaged while printing earlier.

The Re-Print - SALES DC window is as shown.

Slip Details

Slip Number Prefix: Enter the prefix of the Sales DC to be reprinted.

Slip Number: Enter the document number of the Sales DC to be reprinted.

Print: To print the selected Sales DC

Exit: To exit the Re-Print - SALES DC window without printing the details. You can also press Esc to exit from this window.
