Generation of a New Invoice

This option is used to carry out invoicing of goods/ services sold. The invoicing option gives you flexibility in performing activities like suspending the invoice before confirming it and recalling the saved documents while invoicing.

The Invoice window is displayed. The fields displayed are indicative and would depend on the invoicing configuration of your Shoper 9 POS (Distributor).

The details accepted during invoicing can be grouped as shown.

Click the links to learn more about each group.

  • Header: The header part of the invoicing window allows you to accept details like invoice type, transaction type, customer code and sales staff code. Also, the details like invoice prefix, invoice number and invoice date and time are displayed in the header part. Apart from this, the header part allows you to recall the expected transactions (if any).

  • Detail: The detail part of the invoicing window has item details grid as well as direct entry grid. The direct entry grid is used to accept the details stock no., item description, rate and quantity and so on, of each item selected for invoicing. Once the item details of a stock item is accepted through direct entry grid, the same is displayed in the item details grid.

  • Footer: The footer part of the invoicing window displays the invoice totals like total sales value, item level and bill level discounts, total add-ons and deductions, total number of items invoiced and net amount of the invoice and so on. Apart from invoice totals, you are exposed to a separate grid, which gives total sales value, discounts, sales tax, add-ons (if any) and deductions (if any) exclusively.
