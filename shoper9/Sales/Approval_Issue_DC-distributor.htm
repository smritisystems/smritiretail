Approval Issue DC

Approval Issue Delivery Challan (Approval Issue DC) is a document prepared prior to invoicing by the sales staff in a distributor environment. It is used to record the details of the stock items to be sold to a particular customer. The details of Approval Issue DC are recorded by this option. During invoicing, you can recall the Approval Issue DC that is recorded already. The creation of Approval Issue DC of any stock item reduces the stock balance quantity of the item.

The Approval Issue DC is useful in a distributor environment where:

  • Invoicing does not handle merchandise

  • Customer informs the distributor about the required stock items in advance

  • Customer wants to take the stock items before invoicing and if required, make partial returns

The Approval Issue DC in the menu leads to a window that allows various operations related to an Approval Issue DC.

How to reach: Sales > DC Generation > Approval Issue DC

The APPROVAL ISSUE DC window is displayed.

New: To generate a new Approval Issue DC.

Edit: Approval Issue DC cannot be edited.

Void: To delete or cancel previously generated Approval Issue DC.

Reprint: To reprint an Approval Issue DC document.
