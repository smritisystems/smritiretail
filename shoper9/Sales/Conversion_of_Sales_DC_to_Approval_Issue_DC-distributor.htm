Conversion of Sales DC to Approval Issue DC

This option allows you to convert a Sales Delivery Challan to an Approval Issue Delivery Challan.

How to reach: Sales > DC Generation > Conversion of Sales DC to Approval Issue DC

The Sales DC Conversion window is displayed.

Doc Prefix: Enter the document prefix of the Sales DC to be converted to an Approval Issue DC.

Doc Number: Enter the document number of the Sales DC to be converted to an Approval Issue DC.

Doc Date: The date of generation of the selected Sales DC is displayed.  

Customer Details: The customer details of the specified Sales DC are displayed.

Item Details: The details like Stock No, Description, Rate, Quantity and Value of the items in the specified Sales DC are displayed in this grid.

Ok: To convert the selected Sales DC to an Approval Issue DC

Clear: To clear the details entered for fresh entry

Exit: To exit the Sales DC Conversion window
