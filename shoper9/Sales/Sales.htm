Sales

Sales option deals with various sales related operations like invoicing of items, payment of invoices, sales orders for items, and so on, in a distributor environment.

The sales options available are:

  • Invoice/ Return/ Cancellation

  • Sales Order

  • Sales Order Conversion

  • Pending Order/ Trn Closure

Apart from this, in a distributor environment you have an option DC Generation to record delivery challan details.
