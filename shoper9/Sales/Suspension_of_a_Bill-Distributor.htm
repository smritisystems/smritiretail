Suspension of an Invoice

During invoicing at a Distributor location, there would be situations where invoices have to be put on hold. For example, the customer stops the invoicing process midway to add some more items to the cart. In such a scenario the employee at the invoicing counter should be able to suspend the invoice and continue with other invoicing. The suspended invoices can be recalled later.

On Suspension of an invoice, the system generates (configurable) a unique number as Suspended Doc No. and you can recall the suspended invoice with this number to proceed with invoicing.

During invoicing, invoices are suspended by pressing F12 from the Invoice window.

  • The manual entry window for Suspension Document No is displayed.

  • The system generated Suspension Document No (if the corresponding system parameter is set) is displayed.

The Suspension Doc-No can be printed as soon as an invoice is suspended. To enable printing, you need to select the system parameters Print Bill Suspension and POS Printing ON under the category Bill - Printing.

An invoice can be suspended several times. For example, you can recall a suspended invoice, add some more items to it and suspend the invoice once again.

Note: The Suspended Invoice Number can be reused after recalling it for invoicing.
