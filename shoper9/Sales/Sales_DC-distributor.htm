Sales DC

Sales Delivery Challan (Sales DC) is a document prepared prior to invoicing by the sales staff in a distributor environment. It is used to record the details of the stock items to be sold to a particular customer. The details of a Sales DC are recorded by this option. During invoicing, you can recall the Sales DC that is already recorded. The creation of Sales DC of any stock item does not reduce the stock balance quantity of the item.

The Sales DC is useful in a distributor environment where:

  • Invoicing does not handle merchandise

  • Customer informs the distributor about the required stock items in advance

Customer cannot take the stock items out of the Distributor’s premises by producing only a Sales DC. The stock items will be sent out only after invoiced/ converted to an Approval Issue DC.

The Sales DC in the menu leads to a window that allows various operations related to a Sales DC.

How to reach: Sales > DC Generation > Sales DC

The SALES DC window is displayed.

New: To generate a new Sales DC

Edit: To edit a Sales DC

Void: To delete or cancel previously generated Sales DC

Reprint: To reprint a Sales DC document
