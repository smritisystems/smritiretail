Editing a Sales DC

This option allows you to edit a Sales DC in Distributor.

The SALES DC-Edit window is as shown. The fields displayed are indicative and would depend on the slips configuration of your Shoper 9 Distributor.

Click the links to learn more about each group.

  • Header: The details like sales DC prefix, sales DC number and sales DC date and time are displayed in the header part. It allows you to accept details like customer code and sales staff code. Apart from this, the header part allows you to import the details of the items to be included in the sales DC.

  • Detail: The detail part of the sales DC window has item details grid as well as direct entry grid. The direct entry grid is used to accept the details stock no., item description, rate and quantity and so on, of each item selected for editing in the sales DC. Once the item details of a stock item is accepted through direct entry grid and edited, the same is displayed in the item details grid.

  • Footer: The footer part of the sales DC window displays the updated totals like total sales DC value, item level discount, total number of items in the sales DC and net amount of the sales DC and so on. Apart from sales DC totals, you are exposed to a separate grid, which displays the total values of sales DC, discounts, sales tax exclusively.
