Returns without Invoice Reference

This option is used to carry out sales returns of merchandise where the customer who is returning the merchandise does not produce the invoice and if the store policy allows such returns. Here, you can record the sales returns of items without specifying the original invoice details. The window can be invoked either by clicking the button Rtn. W/o ref. in the toolbar or pressing Alt+5 in the Invoice window. The flexibility of entering discounts, add-ons and deductions are also available.

The Return With Out Invoice Reference window is displayed.

Select either Product or Service, as the type of the invoice being returned.

Select either Cash or Credit, as the transaction type of the invoice being returned.

The prefix set for the return, i.e., Return Doc. Prefix is displayed.

The Return Doc. No. is generated automatically.

The current Shoper date and system time are displayed. This is considered as the Return Invoice Date and Time.

Customer: Enter the customer code or press F2 to browse and select the customer code. Recording the customer code helps in generating accurate Customer-wise Offtake Report, as returns should be reduced from the total quantity purchased by the customer.

The Customer Name of the selected Customer Code is displayed.

Sales Staff: Select the sales man ID (configurable) of the salesperson who was involved in the invoicing.

Note: The return will be listed in the sales-man register and the return value will be reduced from the net sale value for the sales staff.

Item Selection: Enter the stock number or scan the item that is to be returned. Select the appropriate Reason Code for the return of the invoice.

Item Details Grid: Displays the details of the items selected for return.

Bill Totals: The total sale value, discount, sales tax, add-ons and deductions (if configured to be captured) are displayed at the bottom right corner of the Return window.

Bill Totals Grid: The details like total quantity of the items selected, total sale value, total tax, total addons and deductions, net amount to be paid to the customer and so on are displayed at the bottom part of the Return window. Each field is explained below.

Total No. of Items: The total number of line items selected for return is displayed in this field.  

Total Qty: The total quantity of items selected for return is displayed in this field.  

Sale Value: The total sale value of the items selected for return is displayed in this field.

Item Level Discount: The total of item level discounts is displayed.    

Bill Discount: The total of bill level discounts (item level discounts are not included) is displayed.

Note: Bill level discount value is appropriated against all the items selected for return.

Total Tax: The total sales tax is displayed. The Total Tax is calculated as follows.

Total Tax = Total Sales Tax on items (both Tax inclusive items and Tax exclusive items) + Tax on Add-ons

Total Addons: The total value of add-ons, if any, is displayed.  

Total Deductions: The total value of deductions, if any, is displayed.

Net Amount: The final amount to be paid to the customer is displayed in this field. The Net Amount is calculated as follows.

Net Amount = (Total Sales value + Total Add-ons + Total Sales Tax) - (Total Discounts + Total Deductions)

The Total Discounts is the sum of Total Item level discounts & Bill level discount.

Net Amount is calculated by rounding off the calculated value to its nearest integer value, if Bill round off is catalogued.
