Goods Outwards based on Stock No.

The record goods issued/ returned are recorded here, based on the stock number.

 How to reach:  Stock > Goods Outwards

 

The Goods Outwards window is displayed. In this window you can execute the following functions.

Add: Click to record goods outwards entry. Click here for more information.

Edit: Click to correct or edit the existing entry. Click here for more information.

Delete: Click to remove an outwards document. Once the grid is populated, click Ok.

Reprint:  Click to reprint an outwards document.

Exit: To quit the Goods Outwards window.

 

Note: Both edit and delete can be done only for documents pertaining to the current Shoper date.

 

Note: the option 1. Load From PDT File is available only with Add option
