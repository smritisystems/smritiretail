Goods Outwards

The Goods Outwards allows recording and updating of transactions such as Purchase Returns, Inter-showroom Transfer-Out, Approval Issues and Miscellaneous Issues.

The details of outward document and the physical quantity of the items despatched / returned can be directly recorded in this option.

 How to reach:  Stock > Goods Outwards

The goods outwards can be accounted in two ways:

  • Based on stock number: You can enter the values in the grid in normal entry mode, by default and the data is accepted SKU-wise, listing each stock number in a row.

  • Based on size-wise: Accepts data in a size grid. When the style-article (SubClass1) and shade (SubClass2) are entered, the sizes applicable get listed. Enter the quantity issued/ returned.

Extension frame work provided from Release 1.3 of Shoper 9 gives a certain degree of customisation in Goods Outwards.
