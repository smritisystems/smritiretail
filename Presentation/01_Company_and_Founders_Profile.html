Slide 3 of 5 • Core Philosophy

Principles: "Less Software is Better Software"

1. Eliminate Operational Friction

Retail systems must prioritize barcode scanners and keyboard shortcuts over dense data entry tables and mouse clicks.

2. Immutable Financial Integrity

Zero unverified ledger edits. Built-in compliance with MCA Section 144 audit log rules and GST statutory checks.

3. 100% Offline Resilience

Sub-urban and Tier-2/3 retail locations must continue billing continuously during internet or network outages.
