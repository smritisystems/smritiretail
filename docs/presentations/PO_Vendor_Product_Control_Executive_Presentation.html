🏛️ Governance Foundation

The Tri-State Procurement Policy Engine

SMRITI replaces opaque spreadsheet rules with an authoritative, database-enforced contract arbitration matrix evaluated instantly upon vendor selection.

🟢 ALLOWED

Primary Vendor Alignment

Directly assigned product on an active supply contract. Line additions proceed instantaneously with zero friction at pre-negotiated purchase terms.

Action: Instant Cart Addition
🟡 CROSS-VENDOR

Regulated Supply Exception

Item primary to another vendor, or secondary assignment. Intercepted by statutory approval dialog requiring 1 of 10 codified business reasons.

Action: Reason Modal Required
🔴 BLOCKED

Impenetrable Guardrail

Product explicitly restricted, uncertified, or blocked for this vendor. Hard-blocked by explanatory modal to eliminate supplier dispute risks.

Action: Strict Prevent Gate
