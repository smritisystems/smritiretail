# ADR-0010: Audit & Timeline Framework

## Status

Accepted

## Decision

All activity history, comments, approvals, and document timelines in SMRITI Retail OS shall be implemented through the shared Audit & Timeline Framework.

## Consequences

- Consistent timeline experience across modules
- Shared comment and activity handling
- Easier reporting and compliance
- Better support for approvals and business history
