# ADR-0016: SCDM v1.0 Architectural Freeze & Platform Capability Roadmap

## Status

Accepted (2026-07-30) — FROZEN v1.0

## Context

Modern Trade customer relationships (e.g. Reliance Retail, D-Mart, More, Large Distributors) require tracking inventory dispatched via GST Sales Invoice that remains at customer locations until sell-out.

Initial proposals evaluated simple consignment or custom UI trackers. However, to maintain SMRITI platform architectural integrity and preserve single-source-of-truth guarantees, an enterprise-grade platform capability was required.

## Decision

1. **SCDM Platform Service**:
   - Establish **SCDM** (SMRITI Channel Distribution Management v1.0) as a Level-1 / Level-2 Platform Service alongside SUNEF, SPF, and SUPOE.
   - Enforce **Rule AOP-004 (Additive Schema Evolution)**, **Rule GR-002 (DRY)**, and **Rule GR-011 (Canonical Ownership)**.

2. **Immutable Single Source of Truth**:
   - Channel stock balance is **NEVER** stored as a mutable running total in a table column.
   - All balance projections are derived dynamically from the `scdm_channel_stock_movements` append-only immutable movement ledger via PostgreSQL view `v_scdm_stock_projection`.

3. **Zero Cross-Contamination**:
   - Warehouse inventory movements (`StockMovement`) and General Ledger accounting entries (`accounting.py`) are **100% isolated** and untouched by SCDM visibility operations.

4. **Event-Driven Coupling**:
   - SCDM dispatches and reversals hook into `Events.SALES_INVOICE_POSTED` and `Events.SALES_INVOICE_CANCELLED` via `SmritiEventBus`. Sales invoice services do not contain inline SCDM logic.

5. **SWSDK v1.0 Capability Registration**:
   - Register `scdm.channel-distribution` workspace bundle into `SWSDKEntrypoint.ts` at SPF Boot Stage 8.

6. **Roadmap & Expansion Boundaries**:
   - **SCDM v1.0 (Frozen)**: Channel Dispatch Engine, Immutable Stock Movement Ledger, Stock Projection View, Sell-Out Ingestion (Excel/CSV/API/Manual), Quantity & Value Reconciliation Matrix, Replenishment Suggestions, Visibility KPIs, SWSDK Studio UI workspace.
   - **SCDM v1.1 Roadmap**: Dedicated Settlement Engine (`SCDMSettlement`, `SettlementDeductions`, `SettlementAdjustments`), Retailer Claims & Deduction Engine, Trade Scheme Recovery.
   - **SCDM v2.0 Roadmap**: Extended Multi-Source Connector Framework (POS Feed, FTP/SFTP, Webhooks, EDI X12/EDIFACT adapters), Secondary Sales Beat Planning, Van Sales Sync.

## Consequences

### Positive
- Prevents cross-contamination with warehouse stock or financial accounting.
- Guarantees complete auditability for all channel stock movements.
- Provides a clean, versioned expansion path without breaking existing contracts.
- Fully integrated with SUNEF navigation, Launchpad tiles, and SWSDK permissions.

### Trade-offs
- Projection queries require aggregation over `scdm_channel_stock_movements` (mitigated by indexing on `(customer_id, product_id, movement_date)` and `v_scdm_stock_projection` view).
