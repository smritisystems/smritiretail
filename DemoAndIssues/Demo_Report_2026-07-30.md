# Demo Report – 2026-07-30

## Module Tested
Launchpad / Sales Studio / POS Entry Experience

## Scenario
Executive-style product walkthrough for a retail ERP evaluation session.

## Steps Performed
- Logged in as the demo administrator
- Navigated to the launchpad
- Reviewed the main navigation modules
- Attempted to access sales-related features
- Reviewed the tenant-assignment guardrail message

## Expected Result
The product should provide a polished, role-aware retail ERP experience with access to sales, POS, and customer modules for the assigned tenant.

## Actual Result
The launchpad presented a professional enterprise experience, but sales-related modules were blocked by a tenant-assignment requirement. The product clearly communicated the access limitation, but it reduced the depth of the demo experience.

## Overall Status
Needs Improvement for full end-user flow, but strong in executive presentation and onboarding aesthetics.

## Notes
- UI presentation is strong and enterprise-ready.
- Business-module access is gated by tenant config.
- The product communicates permissions clearly, but the demo is incomplete without a tenant-scoped operator account.
