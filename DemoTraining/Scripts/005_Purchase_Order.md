# Purchase Order Demo Script

## Introduction
Welcome to the Purchase Order module. In this session, we will learn how to create a purchase order and place it with a supplier.

## Purpose
Purchase orders help the business request goods in a structured and trackable way.

## Business Scenario
A shop needs to restock items from a supplier for the next sales period.

## Navigation
1. Open the Purchase module.
2. Create a new purchase order.
3. Select the supplier.
4. Add products.
5. Review totals and submit.

## Step-by-Step Demonstration
- Enter the purchase order details.
- Choose the supplier.
- Add items to the order.
- Review the total values.
- Submit the order.

## Tips
- Verify supplier and item details before submission.
- Check quantities and pricing carefully.
- Keep purchase history clear and reviewable.

## Common Mistakes
- Sending an order without reviewing the supplier.
- Entering incorrect quantities.
- Missing the final review step.

## Summary
Purchase orders support controlled and organized procurement.
