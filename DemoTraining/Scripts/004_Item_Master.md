# Item Master Demo Script

## Introduction
Welcome to the Item Master module. In this session, we will learn how to manage products and item details.

## Purpose
The item master stores core product information used across purchase, sales, inventory, and reporting.

## Business Scenario
A retail business needs to create and maintain accurate product records for daily operations.

## Navigation
1. Open the Items module.
2. Create a new item.
3. Enter product details.
4. Save and review the record.

## Step-by-Step Demonstration
- Create a new product entry.
- Enter item name, code, and category.
- Save the item.
- Search and review the saved record.

## Tips
- Keep item codes consistent.
- Use clear categories and descriptions.
- Verify pricing and stock details when available.

## Common Mistakes
- Creating duplicate item codes.
- Missing important product details.
- Forgetting to review the item after save.

## Summary
The Item Master module is central to accurate retail and inventory operations.
