# Reports Demo Script

## Introduction
Welcome to the Reports module. In this session, we will learn how to review business performance and operational information.

## Purpose
Reports help managers and decision-makers understand transactions, inventory, and business trends.

## Business Scenario
A manager wants to review sales, stock, or operational results for a period.

## Navigation
1. Open the Reports module.
2. Select a report category.
3. Apply filters.
4. Review the output.
5. Export or print if needed.

## Step-by-Step Demonstration
- Choose the report.
- Apply the relevant filters.
- Review the results.
- Export or print the data.

## Tips
- Use reports to support decisions.
- Filter results carefully.
- Save useful report views for regular review.

## Common Mistakes
- Reviewing the wrong time period.
- Using the wrong filters.
- Forgetting to verify the data source.

## Summary
Reports provide visibility into business activity and help drive actionable decisions.
