# Barcode Demo Script

## Introduction
Welcome to the Barcode module. In this session, we will learn how barcode-based workflows support faster retail and inventory handling.

## Purpose
Barcodes make item tracking and transaction entry faster and more accurate.

## Business Scenario
A store needs to scan items quickly for inventory management or retail checkout.

## Navigation
1. Open the Barcode or inventory workflow.
2. Scan or select an item.
3. Review the captured information.
4. Complete the related action.

## Step-by-Step Demonstration
- Select a barcode-enabled item.
- Scan or enter the barcode.
- Review the item details.
- Complete the action.

## Tips
- Keep barcode labels readable.
- Confirm the item matches the scanned code.
- Use barcode workflows to reduce manual entry errors.

## Common Mistakes
- Scanning the wrong barcode.
- Missing item detail verification.
- Using damaged barcode labels.

## Summary
Barcode workflows improve speed and accuracy in retail and warehouse operations.
