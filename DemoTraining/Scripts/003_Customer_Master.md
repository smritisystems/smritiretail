# Customer Master Demo Script

## Introduction
Welcome to the Customer Master module. In this session, we will learn how to create and manage customer records.

## Purpose
Customer master data supports sales, billing, loyalty, and relationship management.

## Business Scenario
A business wants to record customer details so future sales and communications are accurate.

## Navigation
1. Open the Customers module.
2. Create a new customer.
3. Enter contact and billing details.
4. Save the record.
5. Search and review the customer profile.

## Step-by-Step Demonstration
- Create a new customer record.
- Enter basic information.
- Save the profile.
- Search for the customer.
- Review the record details.

## Tips
- Keep customer names and contact details accurate.
- Use consistent categories and identifiers.
- Maintain clear billing and shipping information.

## Common Mistakes
- Entering incomplete profiles.
- Repeating duplicate customer records.
- Forgetting to verify contact data.

## Summary
The Customer Master module is essential for accurate sales and service processes.
