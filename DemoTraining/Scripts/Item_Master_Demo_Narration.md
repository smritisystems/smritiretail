# Item Master Demo Narration

## Introduction
Welcome to the Item Master module. In this session, we will learn how to create a new product record, enter key item information, and save it in the system.

## Purpose
The Item Master helps businesses maintain accurate product data for sales, inventory, purchasing, and reporting.

## Business Scenario
A retail store wants to add a new product to the system so it can be sold, tracked, and priced correctly.

## Navigation
1. Open the Item Master workspace.
2. Click New SKU.
3. Enter the product name.
4. Enter the SKU or item code.
5. Enter the barcode.
6. Review the form and save the record.

## Demo Steps
- Start from the launchpad.
- Open Item Master.
- Select New SKU.
- Enter the product name such as "BASIC TATTLY THREADS CHAPPAL".
- Enter the SKU or item code such as "CH-01-A".
- Enter the barcode such as "8904551000002".
- Click Save Changes.

## Trainer Notes
- Explain why item master data is important for consistency across the business.
- Highlight the role of SKU, barcode, and product identity.
- Emphasize that clean item data supports purchasing, sales, and inventory accuracy.

## Observed Issue
During the live demo, the save action produced a visible error message: "Save Error" and "Not Found".

## Suggested Training Point
If the system shows an error during save, explain that the product form is available and the business process is understood, but the save operation needs follow-up support or system readiness.
