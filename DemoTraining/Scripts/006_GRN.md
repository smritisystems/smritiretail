# Goods Receipt Note Demo Script

## Introduction
Welcome to the Goods Receipt Note experience. In this session, we will learn how to record goods received from suppliers.

## Purpose
GRN confirms that incoming stock has been received and matches the purchase order.

## Business Scenario
A warehouse team needs to verify goods that arrived from a supplier.

## Navigation
1. Open the purchase or inventory workflow.
2. Select the pending receipt.
3. Review the received items.
4. Confirm quantities and save the receipt.

## Step-by-Step Demonstration
- Open the purchase receipt workflow.
- Review the purchase order details.
- Confirm the received quantities.
- Save the GRN.

## Tips
- Compare received goods with the purchase order.
- Note shortages or damages clearly.
- Keep the stock receipt process accurate.

## Common Mistakes
- Missing quantity mismatches.
- Not recording damaged goods.
- Skipping the verification step.

## Summary
The GRN process helps maintain accurate inventory records and supplier accountability.
