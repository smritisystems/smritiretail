# Sales Invoice Demo Script

## Introduction
Welcome to the Sales Invoice module. In this session, we will learn how to create a sales invoice for a customer.

## Purpose
Sales invoices capture sold products, pricing, taxes, and customer payment information.

## Business Scenario
A retail business wants to issue an invoice after a customer purchase.

## Navigation
1. Open the Sales module.
2. Create a new invoice.
3. Select the customer.
4. Add products.
5. Review totals and taxes.
6. Save or submit the invoice.

## Step-by-Step Demonstration
- Choose the customer.
- Add items to the invoice.
- Review prices and quantity.
- Confirm the total amount.
- Save the invoice.

## Tips
- Verify the customer and products before saving.
- Check pricing and taxes carefully.
- Use the invoice as a clear record of the transaction.

## Common Mistakes
- Selecting the wrong customer.
- Ignoring tax calculations.
- Forgetting to review totals.

## Summary
Sales invoices support accurate billing and financial traceability.
