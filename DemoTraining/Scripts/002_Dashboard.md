# Dashboard Demo Script

## Introduction
Welcome to the dashboard. This is the main control center for users to review operations, alerts, and navigation.

## Purpose
The dashboard helps users quickly understand the state of daily business activity.

## Business Scenario
A store manager wants to review current operations at a glance before moving into specific tasks.

## Navigation
1. Sign in to the system.
2. Open the dashboard.
3. Review the launchpad and application cards.
4. Select a business task.

## Step-by-Step Demonstration
- Review the user workspace.
- Explore the launchpad modules.
- Identify key business functions.
- Open a module from the dashboard.

## Tips
- Use the dashboard to understand priorities.
- Check alerts and messages regularly.
- Keep navigation simple and role-based.

## Common Mistakes
- Overlooking the launchpad structure.
- Selecting the wrong application card.
- Missing role-specific shortcuts.

## Summary
The dashboard provides a fast and organized view of the business environment.
