# Inventory Transfer Demo Script

## Introduction
Welcome to the Inventory Transfer module. In this session, we will learn how to move stock between locations.

## Purpose
Inventory transfers help manage goods across branches, stores, or storage locations.

## Business Scenario
A store needs to move stock from one location to another to meet demand.

## Navigation
1. Open the Inventory module.
2. Create a new transfer.
3. Choose the source and destination locations.
4. Add the items.
5. Save and review the transfer.

## Step-by-Step Demonstration
- Select the source location.
- Select the destination location.
- Add item quantities.
- Review the transfer summary.
- Save the transfer.

## Tips
- Verify stock availability before transfer.
- Confirm the destination location.
- Review transfer history for traceability.

## Common Mistakes
- Sending stock to the wrong location.
- Entering incorrect quantities.
- Forgetting to confirm the destination.

## Summary
Inventory transfers support better stock movement and branch operations.
