# POS Billing Demo Script

## Introduction
Welcome to the POS Billing experience. In this session, we will learn how to process a quick retail transaction.

## Purpose
POS billing helps frontline staff complete fast, accurate sales transactions.

## Business Scenario
A cashier needs to quickly bill a customer and complete the sale.

## Navigation
1. Open the POS workspace.
2. Select or scan the items.
3. Review the bill.
4. Complete the payment step.
5. Finish the transaction.

## Step-by-Step Demonstration
- Add items to the cart.
- Review the totals.
- Apply any necessary payment method.
- Complete the sale.

## Tips
- Keep the flow fast and accurate.
- Confirm the items before checkout.
- Make sure the payment method is clear.

## Common Mistakes
- Missing items from the bill.
- Misreading the total.
- Not confirming the payment step.

## Summary
POS billing supports a smooth customer checkout experience.
